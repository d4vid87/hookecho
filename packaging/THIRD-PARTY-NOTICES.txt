The AppImage includes these voice/audio components:
- Piper 2023.11.14-2 (MIT): https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/tree/2023.11.14-2
- Piper phonemize 1.2.0 (MIT): https://github.com/rhasspy/piper-phonemize/tree/2023.11.14-2
- eSpeak NG (GPL-3.0-or-later): https://github.com/rhasspy/espeak-ng
- ONNX Runtime 1.14.1 (MIT): https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/tree/v1.14.1
- Amy medium voice (CC-BY-SA-4.0): https://github.com/MycroftAI/mimic3-voices
  Voice model revision and checksums: packaging/piper/assets.json in the HookEcho source.
  Piper, eSpeak and ONNX license texts accompany the voice files in usr/lib/hookecho/piper.
- ALSA libasound: see ALSA-copyright in this directory for the packaged version's
  licenses, copyright holders and source URL.

The voice uses English phonemes. Other language dictionaries and the unused Arabic
Tashkeel model are omitted. The English voice model and phoneme rules are unchanged.
