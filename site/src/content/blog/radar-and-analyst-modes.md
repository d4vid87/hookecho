*Updated November 11, 2026 with the current interface captures.*

HookEcho starts with one radar map. The main menu and search open weather layers, point forecasts, storm attributes, sensors, and the product and tool catalog. The playback dock keeps Layout, MRMS, and SPC close to the map.

Choose **Layout → Analysis** in the bottom dock to compare four products. **Ctrl+K** opens search, **Escape** dismisses open menus, and the compact controls keep the site and product in reach.

![Current Radar layout with compact playback controls](/shots/radar-mode-20261111.webp?v=20261111)

When a storm calls for comparison, choose **Layout → Analysis**. Its toolbar holds the presets, pane count, map and time links, active-pane label and Inspector. The four views in this capture show reflectivity, velocity, correlation coefficient and differential reflectivity. The inspector shows the selected data and its actual time.

![Four-pane Analyst Workstation](/shots/analyst-mode-20261111.webp?v=20261111)

The return trip matters just as much. Choose **Layout → Radar** and the single map you left comes back with its site, position, product, threshold, overlays and selected time. Choose **Layout → Analysis** again and its pane layout resumes. Both arrangements live for the session; save a workspace in Tools to keep one across restarts.

These images are from HookEcho itself, replaying the KTLX archive on May 20, 2013. They are examples of the interface, not current weather. [Try the live app](https://app.hookecho.io/) or [follow the step-by-step guide](/docs/radar-and-analyst/).
