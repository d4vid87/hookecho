HookEcho opens in **Radar** mode: one map and a timeline. **Analyst** is an optional workbench for comparing products and inspecting their data. Open **Layout** in the playback dock and select **Analysis** to compare four products; choose **Layout → Radar** to return to one map.

## Use the short menu

The map keeps the main controls within reach:

| Section | What you will find |
| --- | --- |
| **Layout** in the playback dock | Radar, Chase, National overview, Analysis, and pane layouts. |
| **MRMS / SPC** in the playback dock | National radar and Storm Prediction Center outlook controls. |
| **Menu and search** | Weather layers, custom locations, point forecasts, storm attributes, sensors, and settings. |
| **Alerts** badge | The available weather alerts and their details. |

Open Settings from the main menu. Press **Ctrl+S** to open and focus search from anywhere. **Escape** dismisses open menus; it does not change modes.

## Compare a storm in Analyst

1. Select **Layout → Analysis** from the playback dock. Your current Radar view becomes the first pane the first time you enter.
2. The four panels open with different radar products. Use **Options** to adjust the arrangement.
3. Select a pane before changing its radar product or overlays. The toolbar names the active pane.
4. Use **Options** for linked maps and time. Open **Inspector** for product values and the actual source time.
5. Select **Layout → Radar** to return to one map. Select **Layout → Analysis** again to resume the multi-pane arrangement.

![Analyst mode with four radar products](/shots/analyst-mode-20261111.webp?v=20261111)

*KTLX archive replay from May 20, 2013, recaptured November 11, 2026. The panes show reflectivity, velocity, correlation coefficient, and differential reflectivity.*

Your site, map position, product, overlays, threshold settings, and selected live or archive time remain with each mode while the app is open. A disabled threshold remains disabled. Save a **Workspace** in Tools if you want to keep an arrangement across restarts. Multi-pane and explicitly analyst workspaces open in Analyst; ordinary single-pane workspaces open in Radar.

## On a smaller screen

On Android, Signal Deck keeps radar playback visible. The Quiet Shelf opens Radar, Layers, Alerts, and More controls. Use its close control to return to the map. A phone shows one Analyst pane at a time, with a pane picker for moving between them.
