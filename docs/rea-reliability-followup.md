Verification found and corrected two additional glitches. Transition captions originally painted beneath the opaque GPU map; they now paint afterward, and pending stations cannot show a Live badge. The transport omits duplicate source text that overlapped its clock. More substantially, decoded readiness could mask blank panes when queued uploads were not prepared: renderer acknowledgement tokens now gate native/custom radar cache hits and force full retries after eviction or skipped preparation. The browser scenario requires matching requested/prepared tokens in every pane before resource comparisons. A dedicated GPU regression verifies queued versus prepared state, acknowledgement removal on closure, and full reupload after a LUT-only update cannot restore an evicted texture.

Current local validation: 584 HookEcho tests, 428 wxdata tests and the other workspace suites passed; Clippy passed with warnings denied; all 13 native GPU checks passed. The reusable browser scenario passed all eight checks, and rendered station/inspector/transport screenshots were inspected.

The final local browser bundle measured 4,428,702 gzip bytes.
