Hook Echo-WX is a full-fidelity weather radar viewer for storm chasers, spotters and
weather nerds — the same Rust engine as the desktop build, drawing every pixel on the GPU.

It reads the real data, not a rendered picture of it: NEXRAD Level 2 super-resolution
volumes and Level 3 products straight from NOAA's open archives, plus TDWR terminal radars.

Features:

* Level 2 and Level 3 NEXRAD, all 160+ WSR-88D sites, plus 44 TDWR sites
* Every elevation tilt, all-tilts view, and vertical cross-sections
* Reflectivity, velocity, correlation coefficient, differential reflectivity, spectrum width
* Derived fields: VIL, VIL density, echo tops and Witt hail probability
* Storm cell tracking with a sortable storm table
* MRMS national mosaic and QPE, HRRR model fields, GOES GLM satellite lightning
* NWS watches, warnings and advisories, with spoken warning readout
* Winter suite: snowfall accumulation, NOHRSC snow depth, WSSI, mPING reports
* Tropical: wind radii, storm surge, recon HDOBs
* Observations: surface fronts, PIREPs, NDBC buoys, WPC excessive rainfall outlook
* Archive playback back to June 1991, with an animation timeline
* Chase mode: follow your own position, share views by link
* Optional background alerting for saved locations, and a home-screen alert widget

No account, no telemetry, no ads. All data comes from public NOAA/NWS feeds.
